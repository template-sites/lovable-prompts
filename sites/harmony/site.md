## About Page

### Our Story
Harmony Digital Music Academy was founded in 2023 by musicians who believed that geography shouldn't limit your access to great teachers.

We've assembled a team of professional musicians, touring artists, and certified educators from around the world. Our proprietary video platform ensures you get a seamless learning experience - no lag, no buffering, just music.

We use our custom video platform. Just click the link we send you at lesson time - no downloads required.

**What equipment do I need?**
A computer or tablet with a camera and microphone. A decent internet connection. And of course, your instrument!

**Can I switch instructors?**
Absolutely. If it's not the right fit, we'll match you with someone new.
