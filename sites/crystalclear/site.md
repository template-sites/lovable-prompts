## About Page

### Our Story

Crystal Clear Pool Service was founded in 2015 by Mike Chen, a chemical engineer turned pool professional. Frustrated with the "splash and dash" approach of most pool companies, Mike set out to bring scientific precision to pool care.

Today, our team of 8 certified technicians serves over 400 pools across Scottsdale, Paradise Valley, and Fountain Hills. We're proud to maintain a 4.9-star rating across 200+ reviews.

### Our Approach

We believe in data-driven pool care. Every visit includes comprehensive water testing, and our smart monitoring systems track your pool between visits. This means fewer chemical swings, cleaner water, and longer equipment life.
