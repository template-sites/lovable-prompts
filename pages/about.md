## Our Story

PoolPro was founded in 2020 by former pool service operators who were frustrated with outdated software and paper-based systems.

After years of juggling spreadsheets, handwritten route sheets, and chasing payments, we decided to build something better. We started with a simple mobile app for logging service visits, then expanded to include scheduling, invoicing, and customer management.

Today, PoolPro helps over 2,000 pool service companies run more efficiently and profitably. We're proud to serve everyone from solo operators to multi-state enterprises.

## Our Mission

To give pool service professionals the tools they need to run a more organized, profitable business — so they can spend less time on paperwork and more time doing what they love.
