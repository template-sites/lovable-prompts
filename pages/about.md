## Our Story

MusicLessonPro was born out of frustration. Our founder, a piano teacher for 15 years, was tired of juggling spreadsheets, text messages, and payment apps just to run her studio.

Existing software was either too expensive, too complicated, or designed for dance studios and gyms - not music teachers.

So we built something better. Software that understands the unique needs of music education: flexible scheduling for private lessons, practice tracking to keep students engaged, and simple tools that don't require a computer science degree.

Today, MusicLessonPro helps thousands of music teachers spend less time on admin and more time doing what they love: teaching music.

## Our Mission

To give every music teacher the tools to run a thriving studio without the administrative headache.

## Our Values

### Teachers First
Every feature we build starts with a question: "Will this make teachers' lives easier?" If not, we don't build it.

### Keep It Simple
Music teaching is complex enough. Your software shouldn't be. We obsess over making MusicLessonPro intuitive and fast.

### Students Succeed
When students practice, they improve. When they improve, they stay. Our practice tracking features help teachers help students build lifelong musical skills.

## The Team
