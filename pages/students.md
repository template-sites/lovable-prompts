## Add Student Modal

Fields:
- First Name (required)
- Last Name (required)
- Email (required)
- Phone
- Instrument (dropdown: Piano, Guitar, Violin, Voice, Drums, Other)
- Level (dropdown: Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced)
- Lesson Duration (30, 45, 60 min)
- Lesson Frequency (Weekly, Bi-weekly, Monthly, As needed)
- Rate per lesson
- Notes (textarea)
- Parent Information (optional section)
  - Parent Name
  - Parent Email
  - Parent Phone

## Student Detail View

When clicking a student, show:
- Contact information
- Lesson history (last 10 lessons)
- Practice logs (if Pro plan)
- Invoices and payment history
- Notes
- Edit and Delete buttons
