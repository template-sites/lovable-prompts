## Lesson Detail Modal

When viewing a lesson:
- Student name and contact
- Date, time, duration
- Teacher assigned
- Lesson notes (editable)
- Attachments (sheet music, recordings)
- Mark as Completed / No-Show
- Reschedule button
- Add to Invoice button

## Past Lessons Tab

Shows completed lessons with:
- All fields from upcoming
- Status (Completed, No-Show, Cancelled)
- Notes from the lesson
