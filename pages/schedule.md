# Schedule Page

## Page Title
Schedule

## View Toggles
- Day | Week | Month (default: Week)
- Filter by Teacher (if multi-teacher)
- Filter by Instrument

## Click on Lesson
Opens modal with:
- Student name and contact
- Lesson details (instrument, duration)
- Lesson notes
- Reschedule button
- Cancel button
- Mark as completed

## Add Lesson Button (top right)
Opens modal:
- Select student (dropdown)
- Select date and time
- Duration (30, 45, 60 min)
- Recurring (none, weekly, bi-weekly)
- Notes
- Save button

"Set your availability" - links to settings
