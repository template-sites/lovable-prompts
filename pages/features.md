# Features Page

## Hero

**Headline**: Everything you need to run your pool service business

**Subheadline**: From scheduling to invoicing, PoolPro handles it all so you can focus on servicing pools.

## Feature Categories

### Scheduling & Routes

**Route Optimization**
Create efficient daily routes that minimize drive time. Drag and drop to reorder stops. View estimated completion times.

**Recurring Schedules**
Set up weekly, bi-weekly, or monthly service schedules. Automatically generate visits for each pool.

**Calendar Views**
See your entire team's schedule at a glance. Filter by technician, service type, or status.

**Mobile Route View**
Technicians see their daily route on mobile with turn-by-turn directions to each stop.

### Service Logging

**Chemical Readings**
Log pH, chlorine, alkalinity, calcium hardness, and more. Track readings over time with charts.

**Photo Documentation**
Snap before and after photos at each visit. Photos are automatically attached to the service log.

**Task Checklists**
Create custom checklists for different service types. Ensure nothing gets missed.

**Equipment Notes**
Record pump pressure, filter status, and equipment issues. Flag items needing repair.

### Customer Management

**Pool Owner Profiles**
Store contact info, communication preferences, and billing details for each customer.

**Pool Details**
Track pool type, volume, equipment list, gate codes, and access notes.

**Service History**
Complete history of every visit, reading, and photo for each pool.

**Customer Portal**
Customers can view upcoming visits, service history, and pay invoices online.

### Billing & Payments

**Auto-Invoicing**
Generate invoices from completed visits with one click. Include line items for chemicals and add-ons.

**Online Payments**
Accept credit cards and ACH payments. Get paid 2x faster than paper invoicing.

**Recurring Billing**
Set up automatic monthly billing for regular customers.

**Payment Tracking**
See outstanding balances, overdue invoices, and payment history at a glance.

### Team Management

**Technician Profiles**
Add technicians with contact info, certifications, and assigned routes.

**GPS Tracking**
See technician locations in real-time. Know when they arrive and depart each stop.

**Performance Reports**
Track pools serviced, completion rates, and average time per stop for each tech.

### Reporting

**Revenue Reports**
Track monthly revenue, compare periods, and forecast growth.

**Chemical Usage**
Monitor chemical costs and usage trends across all pools.

**Customer Insights**
See which customers are most profitable and which need attention.
