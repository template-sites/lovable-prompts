# Features Page

## Hero
**Headline:** Everything you need to run your music studio

## Feature Categories

### Scheduling & Calendar

#### Smart Scheduling
Set your availability by day and time. Define lesson durations (30, 45, 60 minutes). Block off holidays and vacations.

#### Online Booking
Students book lessons from your custom booking page. No more text message ping-pong.

#### Calendar Sync
Two-way sync with Google Calendar, Apple Calendar, and Outlook. See everything in one place.

#### Automatic Reminders
Email and SMS reminders reduce no-shows. Customize when and how often reminders go out.

#### Makeup Lessons
Track cancelled lessons and schedule makeups. Set policies for how long students have to reschedule.

---

### Student Management

#### Student Profiles
Store contact info, lesson history, instrument, skill level, and notes. See everything at a glance.

#### Parent Accounts
For minor students, parents get their own login. They can book lessons, pay invoices, and view progress.

#### Lesson Notes
Add notes after each lesson. Attach sheet music, recordings, or links. Students access them from their portal.

#### Progress Reports
Generate progress reports for students and parents. Track skills mastered and areas to improve.

---

### Payments & Invoicing

#### Online Payments
Accept credit cards, debit cards, and ACH bank transfers. Powered by Stripe.

#### Automatic Invoicing
Invoices generate and send automatically based on lessons completed. Set your billing cycle.

#### Payment Plans
Let students pay monthly or per-lesson. Set up recurring charges for lesson packages.

#### Late Fee Automation
Automatically add late fees for overdue invoices. Or send gentle reminder emails instead.

---

### Practice Tracking (Pro Feature)

#### Student Practice Portal
Students log their practice time and what they worked on. It takes 30 seconds.

#### Practice Streaks
Gamify practice with streaks and goals. Students stay motivated between lessons.

#### Teacher Dashboard
See who's practicing and who needs encouragement. Spot trends before lessons.

#### Practice Reports
Weekly summaries show practice patterns. Share with parents to celebrate progress.

---

### Studio Management (Studio Plan)

#### Multi-Teacher Support
Add unlimited teachers. Each has their own calendar, students, and payroll tracking.

#### Room Booking
Manage multiple rooms or studios. Prevent double-bookings automatically.

#### Payroll Tracking
Track teacher earnings. Export for easy payroll processing.

#### Custom Branding
Add your logo and colors. Your studio, your brand.
