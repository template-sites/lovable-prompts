## Notifications Tab

### Email Notifications
Toggles for:
- Daily schedule summary
- Payment received
- Invoice overdue
- New lead

### Customer Notifications
Toggles for:
- Service reminder (day before)
- Service completed
- Invoice sent
- Payment receipt

### Save button

## Billing Tab

### Current Plan
- Plan name (Starter, Pro, Team)
- Price
- Next billing date
- Payment method (card ending in XXXX)

Actions:
- Change Plan
- Update Payment Method
- View Billing History

### Billing History
Table:
- Date
- Description
- Amount
- Status
- Invoice link

## Team Tab

### Members
List of team members:
- Name
- Email
- Role (Owner, Admin, Member)
- Actions: Edit role, Remove

### Invite Member
- Email input
- Role dropdown
- Send Invite button

### Roles Explained
- Owner: Full access, billing
- Admin: Full access, no billing
- Member: Limited access
